# Review: The Supply Teacher Who Was LOKI
File: stories/the-supply-teacher-who-was-loki.md | Words: 1,478 | Parts or sections: 10 (all-caps headings)

## Verdicts
- Editor: Funny, clean shape (clues, hamster, mane, apology, fix) and a real moral, but at about 1,500 words it is far under the 4,000 guide; the middle is a list of gags rather than a story, and the mane's fate is never settled. No next-god hook and no "Monday".
- Writer: Lively, quick, good specifics (mirror clock, confused fire extinguisher, "Not bad, brother"). Heavy on ALL-CAPS and "VERY" (a parent will shout half of it), "whispered" repeated, and "Not exactly" three times in the Loki explanation.
- Listener/parent: Warm, funny, and Loki is shown as a friend who learns to say sorry, with Thor dignified and not mocked. The ending (fish in the fountain, "We never did") is playful rather than sleepy; fine, but not a wind-down.

4. The Hamster: OLD: "Biscuit was a pretty big hamster, too. A little bit **bigger**. And he winked." -> NEW: "And he was a little bit **bigger** than Biscuit. And he winked." (removes the muddled "pretty big ... a little bit bigger")

## ASK fixes (change plot, a character's arc, an ending, a joke that matters, the hook)
1. The Ending: no hook for the next god and no twins saying "Monday" (standing rule). Suggest one line after "We never did." that points to a new staff member (as Bible order into later stories requires), ending "Monday" if that fits the early-class set-up. Check the Bible's "How the early staff fit later canon" for what story 7 expects.
2. Who is Loki: "Felix Bailey" is a pupil here, and the twins are Alex and Felix. If this is meant to be a different Felix, consider renaming (e.g. "Finn Bailey") to avoid confusion with the later cast.
3. Mr Locke's Plan/The Fixing: he says he only "borrowed" the mane and "was going to put it back", then makes a new one. Where is the real wool mane? Suggest one line (found in his bag, or he hands it back) so the loose end is closed.
4. Length: at 1,500 words it could take one more full beat, such as a second prank that goes wrong before the mane (the twins/class trying to catch him out), but this is the author's call; the story is tight and does not sag.

## Minor notes
