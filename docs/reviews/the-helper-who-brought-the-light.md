# Review: The Helper Who Brought the Light
File: stories/the-helper-who-brought-the-light.md | Words: ~4,200 (4,260 with front matter) | Parts: 16

## Verdicts
- Editor: Strong shape. Clear problem (the dark), a good echo of the myth (boxes within boxes, baby trick, chimney, black feathers), and a hook (Coyote and the howl) that is a real mystery. The middle holds. The weakest stretch is Parts 13 to 15, where the "light is shared" idea is stated four or five times (Alex, Felix, Gloam's speech, Raven's speech, Pearl).
- Writer: Voice and jokes are specific (the spoon, "Id'd da ony way ow", the tolerates gag, Willow and the spark). Sentences read aloud cleanly. Minor tics: "at exactly the same time" used outside the ritual in Part 14, a few "That's when" (Parts 5, 8, 13, plus "that was the moment" in 9, so at the limit). A few words are a stretch for a six-year-old ("mischievous", "magnificent", "superior").
- Listener/parent: Warm, funny and safe. The dark in Part 5 is scary but resolved fast by Raven. Gloam's grief ("since my wife went away") is gentle and Pearl's line about feelings is lovely. Ends with a howl that bristles Willow, a small jolt, but the closing image of a sky full of stars and the twins saying "Monday" settles well. Respectful to the tradition: Elfine places Raven in Haida and Tlingit lands with dignity, and Raven is clever and kind, never mocked.

## Twin balance
Alex: ~13 spoken lines; names the raven, asks "did he cry for the box?", says the sharing-candle idea, "Come in!" | Felix: ~12 spoken lines; explains Alex's answer, spots the smile, tells the old man he is scared too | Goes first: Felix (steps through the door in Part 8; Alex follows). Verdict: Balanced. Felix opens the heart of the scene, Alex gives the argument, and both get a "Come in!" and a share of the raven's praise. Willow's brave act is the spark catch (Part 11) and she never speaks.

## ASK fixes (change plot, a character's arc, an ending, a joke that matters, the hook)
1. Parts 14 and 15: The moral is stated by Gloam's speech ("light isn't something you hold, it's something you share"), then again by Raven ("the best part wasn't the trick. It was the sharing"), after Alex, Felix and Pearl already said it in Part 9 and 13. Suggest cutting Gloam's speech to two short lines, or trimming Raven's to "Kindness turned the key" and "Brave is going in anyway, holding someone's hand". The party stays; the repetition goes.
2. Part 15: "Brave isn't never being scared" is a stock sunset-speech line and a double negative that is hard for a young listener to parse. Suggest "Brave is being scared and going in anyway, holding someone's hand."
3. Part 6: Raven lists the helpers he needs (climber, story-knower, sneaker, "two people who can talk"). Dot is brought along but has no task there; her big moment is spotting the house (Part 5) and the shout in Part 12, which is fine. Consider a one-line job for her, or leave as is.
4. Part 16: The Coyote howl ends the night on a bristling Willow. Optional: add one soft closing line after "Monday." is not possible (the ending is fixed), so instead soften the howl earlier ("a long, wild, happy howl") to keep bedtime calm.
