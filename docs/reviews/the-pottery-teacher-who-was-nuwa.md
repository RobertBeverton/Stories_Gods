# Review: The Pottery Teacher Who Was NÜWA
File: stories/the-pottery-teacher-who-was-nuwa.md | Words: about 4,450 | Parts or sections: 24 (all-caps headings)

## Verdicts
- Editor: Sound shape and right length. Clear problem (the old myth, retold with a flagpole), a good five-stones quest with two stones each, a strong turn where Zhu and Gong mend what they broke, and Ao's "slow and steady" carries the whole story. The middle does not sag. The hook is thin: Yu arrives and Maya names him, but there is no question to wonder about overnight.
- Writer: Clean, speakable, funny in places (MR ZHU, KITCHEN (HOT); "Mr Harris never notices anything that doesn't have a clipboard"). The "He ran." refrain is a good device. "Looked at each other" is overused (6 times). Six affirmations is too many, and the last four are recited in a row.
- Listener/parent: Warm, dignified god; Ms Wa is never mocked. The climb is the one tense stretch, and it is relieved by the twins' mantras and Dot peeking through her fingers. The yellow-pebble girl ("I've got my coat") is the best moment. Ends on gold light and a rainbow seam, which is settling, and Yu at the gate is a gentle lift rather than a jolt.

## ASK fixes (change plot, a character's arc, an ending, a joke that matters, the hook)
1. THE WHITE STONE: Arabella Webb is the Pridefield Primary team captain and the boastful Arachne from story 14 (an outside visitor). Here she is a Year 6 pupil at this school sitting on "our marker" and says "It's our marker. We've had it for years." Continuity slip. Suggest swapping her for Gideon Fox (Year 6 here, stories 10 and 13), or keep Arabella but make the stone the Pridefield team's, and let her pay off her story-14 boast ("I don't know what I'm doing" is a nice echo of Athene's "I don't know yet"). Needs a decision.
2. THE LAST WORDS: six affirmations (scared and brave; I can do hard things; Stop, breathe, I am safe; I am not alone; There is always hope; I know you can). The last four are recited back to back after the sun goes down, which reads as a list. Suggest keeping the two earned on the climb and one at the end, and dropping at least two of "I am not alone", "I know you can" (Dot's line has no set-up).
3. THE ENDING: Yu is announced, not hooked. Suggest one added image or question (e.g. he looks at the seam in the sky and says "That is not how I would have mended it," or he asks where his father is, tying to Mr Gun in story 18) so there is something to wonder about before "Monday".
4. THE TWINS CLIMB: both twins climb a wet flagpole under a crack with something "enormous, and not entirely friendly" behind it. Stock "child in danger at height" beat and the scariest line in the story. Consider softening "not entirely friendly" to something odder and less menacing (e.g. "enormous, and very, very curious").
