# Review: The Librarian Who Was ANUBIS
File: stories/the-librarian-who-was-anubis.md | Words: about 1,920 | Parts or sections: 12 (plain all-caps headings)

## Verdicts
- Editor: Clean, funny week-long structure (theory, evidence, mummy, Gary, feather test, phone call, hook) and every section pays off. But at about 1,900 words it is under half the usual length and feels brisk rather than rich. Affirmation ("Accidents don't count... your heart is light") is short and plot-earned. Hook (the bird-like dinner lady = Thoth) is a real mystery. No "Monday" ritual, which is expected for an early story.
- Writer: Strong, specific child voice and good jokes (cheese and onion crisp, triangle hair, "Obviously."). Sentences read aloud easily. Heavy use of ALL CAPS for emphasis and "We all..." openers; fine for a parent, but trim if narrated by one voice. "That's when" appears twice, which is fine.
- Listener/parent: Gentle and safe for a six-year-old. Death is handled with warmth (Gary's burial, "a kind fish"), the weighing of hearts is reassuring ("if you are mostly kind"), and Anubis is dignified and kind, never mocked. One small wince: a child wrapped so his mouth is covered. Ends on a smile, and settles well.

## Twin balance
Not applicable (twins not in this story; class of Nell, Kofi, Archie, Freddie, Priya, with an unnamed "we" narrator). Nell and Kofi share the dialogue evenly.

## ASK fixes (change plot, a character's arc, an ending, a joke that matters, the hook)
1. Length: about 1,900 words against a guide of roughly 4,000. Optional expansion if wanted: one more evidence beat or a second "test" before the feather. Not needed for quality, only for series consistency.
2. Hook: Nell says "I think we might meet him next" about Thoth, but the next disguise is a woman (Mrs Ibbs in the Bible). Consider "meet him (or her) next" or "meet Thoth next". Low priority, and it fits the running disguise gag.
