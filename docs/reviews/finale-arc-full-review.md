# Finale arc (stories 40 to 47): full read-through

> **Status: fixes applied 2026-11-13.** All eight chapters were re-read in full, in order, against the Bible, Archive and the earlier stories (keys and locking from story 38, Hanuman from story 12, Gary from story 7, the cat flap from story 39, Gary from the Anubis story). Reviewed by me alone, with no subagents. One persona round, one adversarial round, fixes, then a grep pass for tics, ages and "Saturday". The earlier per-chapter checks used a continuity ledger; this is the first time every chapter was read end to end after the last one was written.

## Verdicts

- **Alex and Felix.** Speaking lines level in every chapter (Alex/Felix: 20/17, 18/20, 30/28, 23/25, 24/26, 15/14, 13/13). Ch45 is the school side, so the twins speak only through their two letters (Alex and Felix write alternately, equal length) and the dawn scene in Part 13. That is by design. Big moments are split: Felix asks "Has anyone asked him?" (44), Alex coaches Tyr ("Not yet", "Say it like a friend", 46), both say sorry-first and the keys (46).
- **Five-year-old listener.** Scariest beats (Odin's wolves, Hel's house, Tyr's hand, the puppy jumping at the moon) each land on a laugh within a few lines. Nothing unfair is left unfixed. The three "same game, same words, same breath" explanations (41, 43, 45) are deliberate repetition, short each time.
- **Parent reading aloud.** Hard names now have respellings. The longest run-on (Frigg's arrival in 47) was split into short paragraphs. Notes on Willow's collar are capitals and short.
- **Sensitivity (Norse, Inca, Hindu cameo).** Hel's house is gentle and respectful ("resting", "finished"). Fenris is not bound, and Maya's "the end of the book hasn't happened yet" flags the change. Tyr's hand is told plainly and gently. Quilla's silver tears and calendar are accurate. Mister Hanu's "tried to eat the sun" echoes his own story with care.
- **Editor.** Shape holds across the eight nights; each ends on a reason to turn the page and the finale ends on rest. Tension types vary. Quiet beats follow the scary ones (Mister Harris on the step in 45, the twins leaning on Tyr in 44, the walk home in 46).

## What the full read found, and what was done

**Real continuity breaks (fixed)**
1. **Willow's crossings (44 and 45).** Ch44 had Maya's note arrive on Wednesday at dusk, but ch45 had Willow first reach the school on Thursday with the twins' letter, so Maya's note could not have been written. Now: Wednesday home time she crosses to the school, Maya and Hel write the note (Hel's wobbly P.S.), she returns at dusk; Thursday dawn she brings the twins' letter, which now says thank you for the note. Hel's P.S. in ch44 is now in her own voice ("I WILL TRY. HEL.").
2. **Why Friday (45).** The school knew the rule on Tuesday, so why wait? Quilla now explains she is only halfway in each sky until she is full, and tomorrow night is the first time she can say "Now" to both.
3. **The moon rising (46).** She had been sitting on the roof all week and then "rose behind the hill". Now she lifts herself off the roof "like somebody getting out of a very deep armchair".
4. **Janus's five days (47).** The last day is the Friday after the hill, so he has been standing twelve days. Added that he let go of the clock the moment the door was locked and kept standing because no chair was wide enough.
5. **Loki's "Saturday" (46).** The thin place is locked after the climax and only opens with the keys, so a Saturday visit was impossible. Now "the very first time I'm allowed", which pays off at noon on the last day.
6. **"Hel's room you landed in" (42), "Nana's landing light" (42), "since Monday night", "Mister Janus said they'd open any door" (41).** Wrong or invented. Fixed to what the text and story 38 actually say.
7. **Ages (41).** Two leftovers ("five or six", "He was five") removed.
8. **"Hel will put a cushion on" the chair (46).** The chair already had a star cushion (43). Now "I will dust it first", and in 47 she says she has.
9. **47 played the game "on the bump" on top of the hill.** Fixed, and Maya now asks whether playing the same game on two hills is safe. Mrs Nuwa: a seam only swaps when it is left alone; somebody is minding each end. This was the biggest adversarial hole.
10. **"Coat peg talked out of leaving it" (47)** read backwards. Fixed.
11. **"The book hasn't happened yet" (45)** clashed with Tyr's hand already lost. Now "the end of the book".
12. **Ch43's Part 1 recap** said the twins knocked; it was Willow. Fixed. The cryptic "it's his moon, actually" removed.
