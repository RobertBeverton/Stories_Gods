# Review: The Lady Under the Floor

> **Status: ASK fixes applied (story 35, about 4,100 words, 16 parts).** The DIRECT fixes below were applied in the first pass; the ASK items were worked through in the third pass. The quotes and part names in this report describe the text as it was before that pass. Dot's rescue gets a line and the 'second best thing' pays off (chips), the digger driver apologises and offers to help, the hook no longer carries the hundred days (story 36 introduces it), 'Mister Ketz' spelling fixed.
>
> Weak spots recorded after the fix pass: None significant.

## Verdicts
- Editor: Clean recipe and a clear problem that echoes the myth (no thank-you, so the ground shakes; ayni sets it right). Middle holds up: digger, small shakes, crack, apology, gift, rescue of the digger, new plan, feast. The affirmation ("You are held" and "A thank you is a seed") is earned. The hook (bear and tiger who want to be people) is a real mystery and "Monday" lands for both twins. Part 12 is a little talky, and Dot on the edge of a crack is a stock beat.
- Writer: Voice is warm and funny (carrot growing the wrong way, "rather smug" cabbages, apologetic raindrop, "the only potato you've ever eaten"). Few tics: "looked at each other" x3, "exactly the same time" once outside the ritual, "That's when" once. A few long sentences, and the country list in Part 3 is hard to say aloud.
- Listener/parent: Gentle, funny, and settles well. The quiet Parts 14 and 15 are good lullaby material. Tradition treated with warmth and dignity (libation and sulpayki framed as a living practice). Crack and screaming driver are scary for a moment but relieved at once by Part 11.

## Twin balance
Alex: ~14 spoken lines; catches Dot's left hand, digs the hole, leads the "ask and say sorry" idea | Felix: ~16 spoken lines; catches the right hand, plants the potato, says "Come on" and "Listen" | Goes first: Felix (first to speak in Part 1; "Listen" opens the hook). Verdict: balanced. Willow's one brave act (biting both coats) is in the right place, and she stays silent.

## ASK fixes
1. Part 7: The Dot-on-the-breaking-ground rescue is the stock "small child in danger" beat. It does give Willow her moment and both twins equal action, so keep it. If wanted, give Dot a small joke or contribution while hanging on, so she is not only rescued. Her "second best thing" line in Part 8 is cute but unclear (what is the best?).
2. Part 12: Pachamama grants the Sports Hall on the car park with no cost to Mister Harris. It is a fair solution, but the digger driver is never mentioned again. Optional: one line in Part 12 or 13 of the driver coming back to say sorry or to help with the compost.
3. Part 16: The bear and tiger hook is a good mystery, but they are a lot of exposition ("a hundred days", garlic, leaves) for one paragraph. Consider holding back the "hundred days" detail so the final beat is cleaner. Only change if the next story needs it.
