# Review: The PE Teacher Who Was HANUMAN
File: stories/the-pe-teacher-who-was-hanuman.md | Words: about 2,950 (story text) | Parts or sections: 20 (all-caps headings)

## Verdicts
- Editor: Warm, well-shaped and the myth echo is strong (forgotten power, a friend reminds him, a fetch-the-herb quest, Jambavan mirrored by the twins). But it is about 1,000+ words under the 4,000 guide, and the front half is lecture: Maya's myth (Parts 2-4) plus the evidence list say the same thing twice. The Sleep/Moonbloom problem is imported from the Felicity/Fortune story and arrives suddenly at Sports Day. No "next god" hook and no "Monday" ending (the closing letter and mango-race P.S. are lovely but the series ritual is missing).
- Writer: Clean, funny, easy to read aloud. Strict "Alex said / Felix said" alternation in the remembering scene is mechanical but works by ear. "looked at each other" turns up three times; narrator "we" drifts into omniscient on the hill (twins alone with Mr Hanu). A few small clumsy lines (see fixes).
- Listener/parent: Gentle, funny and satisfying; the swan, the compost bin and the mountain asking permission are bedtime gold. Ends calm (moonlight, letter). Respect for Hanuman, Rama and Sita is good overall: the god is brave, loving and dignified once he remembers, Rama and Sita appear quietly and kindly, devotion ("I do things for him because I love him") is treated sincerely. A few early slapstick gags are the only soft spot (see ASK 2).

## Twin balance
Alex: about 18 spoken lines, leads the questions, the list pencil, "Stop. Breathe.", calls "Got it!" | Felix: about 17 spoken lines, "You found Sita", asks the mountain nicely, spots Maya's compost bin | Goes first: Alex (whispers "He grew" first). Verdict: balanced. The twins pick the flower together and share every big moment. Slight pattern: Felix is the one who wobbles on the hill and Alex steadies him; fine here, but watch it across stories.

## DIRECT fixes (safe to apply without asking)
1. Part "SPORTS DAY": OLD: "On Friday, it was Year 1 Sports Day." -> NEW: "On Friday, it was Sports Day." (age-proofing; the story keeps its sense)
2. Part "THE HILL"/"THE PROBLEM": OLD: "They both reached out one hand each, and they picked it together, very gently," -> NEW: "They each reached out one hand, and they picked it together, very gently," ("both ... each" is clumsy aloud)
3. Part "THE TWINS REMEMBER"/"THE LEAP": OLD: "over the swan, who looked up in astonishment, and over the dark trees at the far side." -> NEW: "over the swan, who looked up in surprise, and over the dark trees at the far side." (easier word for a young listener)
4. Part "THE MOUNTAIN": OLD: "Alex and Felix looked at each other.\n\nAlex said, \"We're sorry. We should have asked.\"" -> NEW: "Alex and Felix said nothing for a moment.\n\nAlex said, \"We're sorry. We should have asked.\"" (trims the "looked at each other" tic; this is one of three uses)
5. Part "THE FRIEND HE SERVES": OLD: "a kind and brave prince who he helped, and who he loves more than anyone." -> NEW: "a kind and brave prince. He helped him once, and he loves him more than anyone." (smoother to say in one breath)
6. Part "THE LESSON": OLD: "Alex said, \"Like you reminded us at the Pan play.\"" -> NEW: "Alex said, \"Like we reminded each other at the Pan play.\"" (Mr Hanu was not in that story, so "you" is a continuity slip; check that this reading suits story 11 before applying, else drop the line)

## ASK fixes (change plot, a character's arc, an ending, a joke that matters, the hook)
1. Ending: no hook for the next god and no "Monday" said by both twins at once, which the standing ending requires. Suggest: after the P.S., a short beat (a new sound at the gate, a clue) and the twins saying "Monday" together, then "The End." Check the Archive for what story 13 picks up, so the hook matches it.
2. Parts 1-2 and "THE PROBLEM WITH MR HANU": respect. Hanuman is hung upside down eating a banana, waves with his foot, wears a bib back to front, blows himself into beanbags, and the P.S. is a "monkey scrawl". Affectionate, and it is the forgetting-spell that explains the silliness, so mostly fine. Gentlest fix: drop the bib gag and keep the banana and upside-down gags, so the god's comedy comes from his forgetfulness, not from monkey mimicry.
3. Length and shape: ~1,000+ words short and front-loaded with Maya's retelling. Suggest cutting the duplicated bits of "THE LIST" and using the space for one more beat of Mr Hanu failing at PE (a funny set piece), and a short build before the Sleep arrives, e.g. a hint on Thursday that Ms Fortune's wheel is spinning oddly. This makes the SLEEP problem feel seeded rather than dropped in.
4. Willow does not appear at all. Per the standing rule she should do one brave thing (never speaking). Suggest: Willow guards Mrs Felicity on the sofa, or fetches the Moonbloom seed pot, or walks across the drained field to the gate. Small and easy.
5. Cast overlap: Ms Fortune is "from PE" while Mr Hanu is "our new PE teacher". Not contradictory (Bible lists her as PE), but a one-line acknowledgement (e.g. "Ms Fortune, who shares the PE cupboard") would stop a parent wondering. Also the promised "new seed in return" for the mountain is never delivered; add one line in the Sports Day ending (a seed planted by the gate) so the promise is kept.

## Minor notes
- "We all looked" appears three times in Parts 3, 6 and 7; vary one.
- Narrator "we" cannot see the hill scenes; one line like "Alex told us afterwards" in Part "THE HILL" would keep the child narrator consistent.
- "wiry" in the opening description may be unfamiliar; "small and quick" would do.
- Three affirmations plus the new "I KNOW YOU CAN" is a lot; "I KNOW YOU CAN" is the earned one and sits well at the end. Fine as is.
- Good touches to keep: the mountain asking permission and accepting sorry, the swan's continuing role, "You were equal, and you were right."
