# Review: The Fire Lady and the Lollipop Lady (rewrite pass)
File: stories/the-fire-lady-and-the-lollipop-lady.md | Words: about 4,150 (4,221 by wc including headings) | Parts: 15

## Verdicts
- Editor: No longer thin. The middle holds: 4 lava, 5 rescue, 6 Namaka, 7 failed attempt, 8 the twins' fix, 9 canoe, 10 Willow, 11 sorry, 12 islands. All three new parts earn their place. Part 3 plants the toast, the bucket, the "calm down" trigger and Felix's pocketed slice, and Part 14 pays all four off. Part 7 (each twin picks a side and gets singed or soaked) is now the best idea in the story. Weak spots: Part 13 recites the moral just before Part 14 acts it, and Pele never actually asks for help during the crisis.
- Writer: The new parts match the old voice well (short, concrete, deadpan jokes such as "Population, one worm", "walked into the door"). Tics are mild: "looked at each other" appears once (Part 7) and "at exactly the same time" only in the Monday ritual. Soft repeats are "wobbly" three times (Parts 9, 12, 14), "went (a bit) pink" twice (Parts 3, 14), and a lot of "slowly" and "whispered". No year groups or ages are named (Reception and Willow's age are fine).
- Listener/parent: Warm and funny, and the toast and breathing ending settles well. The slice of bread is the child-sized thread through the story. The Part 13 stillness plus Part 14 comedy is a good wind-down, with the Part 15 hook to finish on.

## Continuity check
- Monday afternoon lesson (3), Tuesday lava (4-13), "next morning" Wednesday toast (14), Friday hook (15): all consistent.
- Felix's slice: pocketed Monday, produced Wednesday. He was soaked by Namaka's mist on Tuesday (Part 7), so a dry "squashed" slice is a slip (see fix 5).
- Mister Harris's bucket: thrown in Part 3, "empty bucket" joke, then never seen again (see ASK 4).
- Nana: "Nana taught it to us" (14) and "before their nana arrived" (15) agree. The trick is not planted earlier (see ASK 3).
- Willow's position: under the bench (7), then "climbing frame bench" (10). Minor (fix 6).
- "Three weeks": Harris says the council needs three weeks (3) and Namaka has searched for three weeks (6). Accidental echo (fix 4).
- Who is present: fine. Elfine and Marion absent from Parts 3 and 14 is plausible. Mister Harris vanishes in 6-10 and returns with Dot on his shoulders in 11; fine.

## ASK fixes (change plot, a character's arc, an ending, a joke that matters, the hook)
1. Part 13: The affirmation is stated twice here ("Having big feelings isn't bad" from Alex, then Pele's "It's what you do with them that counts") and then acted out in Part 14. Per the owner's preference, cut Alex's line and Pele's "It's what you do..." sentence. Keep Pele's "I always thought that made me a bad goddess" / Felix's "It doesn't" and "I didn't know I was allowed to ask", then let Part 14 carry the moral. The Marion/Elfine bathroom joke can stay as the button.
2. Part 10: The lesson is "stop and ask for help", but Pele never asks. Elfine arranges Willow for her. Give Pele one line after "she'll put me out", such as "Could somebody help me? I don't know how to stop." Then Elfine's "Willow's going to help" answers a request instead of a diagnosis.
3. Parts 8 or 7: Nana's breathing trick turns up from nowhere in Part 14. Plant it once earlier, for example as the twins walk between the goddesses, Alex squeezes Felix's hand and they whisper "Smell the toast" (his fringe still smells of it), so Part 14 is a payoff and the twins act the affirmation themselves.
4. Part 14: Bucket callback missing. Have Mister Harris peek in holding a full bucket behind his back, and set it down quietly when Pele says "Thank you, Mister Harris." One line, kind joke, and the bucket thread is closed.
5. Part 10: Willow's brave beat is thin (she is carried and purrs). Add one beat of hesitation, such as looking at the lava, then at Pele, before she settles, since she was hiding with paws over her nose in Part 7.
6. Part 8: Title "Alex and Felix Step In" overlaps with Part 7 (where they already stepped in). Consider "Nobody's Side".

- Front matter `characters` lists Elfine and Marion but omits Maya, Kenji, Ollie and Mister Harris, who all appear. The Bible gives Maya the explainer job but Elfine does it here; fine for this era.
- Part 5: "with Marion and the twins holding on to her" while they all hang by their hands is physically odd. A line like "cheering her on" fixes it.
- Hard words for a six-year-old: prow, billow, population (the last is rescued by the joke). "Slowly" and "whispered" are overused; thin them in a later polish.
