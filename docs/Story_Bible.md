# Story Bible

The standing facts for this series. Read this first, then `Story_Archive.md`, then `God_Tracker.md`. The full text of the stories is canon; this is the quick index to it.

## Premise

Alex and Felix Bailey are twin boys, aged 5, in Year 1. Each story, a god or legend from a different world mythology turns up as a new member of school staff. The children work out who they are, and help them. The stories are bedtime stories, read aloud, about 4,000 words each.

**A note on the myths.** These stories are based on existing myths and legends, retold for young children with our own spin: a school setting, gentler endings, invented details and jokes. They are not authoritative retellings. Where a story departs from the traditional tale, that is deliberate. For the real stories, look at the traditions themselves, and treat gods from living religions with care.

## The cast

**Primary (every story)**
- **Alex and Felix**, the twins. Equal weight, always: equal lines, equal contributions, big moments split between them or done together. They say "Monday" at the end of every story at exactly the same time.
- **Willow**, the Bengal Reading Cat of Year One, aged 9. Dignified, deadpan, never speaks, does one brave thing per story.
- **Maya Singh**, the explainer. Bookish classmate with a notebook and a pencil. Collects "evidence", works out who the god is, tells the myth, underlines the big idea three times.

**Tertiary (as needed)**
- **Dot** (Reception, the youngest, fearless, loves lost property and dancing), **Kenji** (first to ask questions), **Ollie** (always hungry).
- **Mister Harris**, the head teacher. Flustered, kind, has a clipboard.
- **Missus Tibbs**, the dinner lady (in the early stories). **Nana**, the twins' grandmother (cameo).

**Elfine and Marion** (twin cousins, aged 8) appeared in stories 3 to 11 (Anansi to Father Frost). They were retired at the user's request from story 12 (Bast) onwards. Do not include them unless asked.

If the cast changes, the user will say so. Keep the latest instruction until told otherwise.

## Standing requirements

- Alex and Felix get equal weight.
- Reading level Year 2 to 3, school reference Year 1.
- Affirmations are woven into the narrative. No notes page, no questions for the children afterwards.
- Each story ends with a hook for the next god, and the twins say "Monday" in unison.
- About 4,000 words. (Stories run longer or shorter as they need to.)
- Keep sensitive subjects kid-appropriate. Moloch was declined because of child sacrifice. Living religions are portrayed warmly and respectfully.
- Each story uses a different tradition, myth and moral.

## Running threads and established facts

- **Soup Club** runs on Fridays in the dinner hall, with Baba Yaga cooking and Dot as her assistant.
- **Gobbler Hill** is the hill behind the school. Coyote's story woke it; it is now round, green, full and sleepy.
- **The Playground Committee** and the rebuilt playground: Oshun's golden pond, a tower with a bell, and a bench with the plaque "There is always room for one more."
- **Lost Property** is the cupboard under the stairs, now run by Madam Simurgh. (Maui's badge also says Lost Property. Treat it as a shared old joke, or say she took it over.) The twins hold a feather between them: it only works if both hold it and say "We're lost, please."
- **The patchwork seam across the sky** from Nuwa's mending glows in every colour at sunrise and sunset.
- **Amaterasu's storeroom** at the end of the caretaker's shed has its stone door wedged open on purpose. Eight hundred tiny giggling lantern spirits (kami) came with her.
- **Pachamama** lives under the vegetable patch. The school field is now a meadow and garden. Everyone gives a spoonful of thanks to the ground at lunch. Leftover food goes in the compost. The Sports Hall is to be built on the car park. The llama with a golden ear stayed with her.
- **Hwanung's cellar** (the old coal cellar) was the bear's cave. The tiger is the Playground Guardian. Mrs Ungnyeo has the porridge.
- **Idun's apples:** the twins planted two apple pips side by side in the school orchard. Thiazi is the Sky Postman. The Norse staff are back to normal age.
- **Father Frost** and Miss Snow have a snow house.
- **Janus's keys:** Alex and Felix each hold one of Mister Janus's two-toothed golden keys and lock the front doors each night: Alex says goodbye to the day and turns his key, Felix says hello to tomorrow and turns his. The green door from the field now stands beside the playground like a gate. Willow once held the great front doors open by refusing to be in or out.
- Mister Ebbie and his boat-bus appear in story 1.
