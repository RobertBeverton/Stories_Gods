# God Tracker

## Next up

**Brigid** (Irish/Celtic): goddess of fire, wells and poetry. The hook at the end of story 20 shows a white cow with red ears, a rush cross with a flame that does not burn it, and a rhyming voice in the dark. A school job might be Poetry and Wells, Library, or Warm Hands. Gentle myth ideas: the cloak spread over a field, the cow that fed the poor, the cross woven from rushes, a hearth fire that never goes out. Let Alex lead.

## Ideas not yet used

- Brigid (Irish): fire, wells and poetry.
- Hanuman (Hindu): leaping, strength, and loyalty.
- Inti or Mama Quilla (Inca): sun and moon.
- Rainbow Serpent (Aboriginal Australian): only with great care and respect for a living tradition.
- Thor in his own story (Norse).
- Ganesha (Hindu): beginnings and obstacles. A second Hindu tradition after Lakshmi, so only if the user asks.
- Anubis or Thoth (Egyptian).
- Sedna (Inuit): only in a heavily softened form.
- Tengri (Mongolian): the sky.
- Mami Wata, Papa Legba, Yemaya: only with care for living traditions.

## Considered and declined

- **Moloch**: sources associate him with child sacrifice, so he was declined as unsuitable for children.
