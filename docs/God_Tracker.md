# God Tracker

## Next up

**Brigid** (Irish/Celtic): goddess of fire, wells and poetry. This will be story number 39. The hook at the end of story 38 shows a white cow with red ears, a rush cross with a flame that does not burn it, and a rhyming voice in the dark. A school job might be Poetry and Wells, Library, or Warm Hands. Gentle myth ideas: the cloak spread over a field, the cow that fed the poor, the cross woven from rushes, a hearth fire that never goes out. Let Alex lead.

## Ideas not yet used

- Brigid (Irish): fire, wells and poetry.
- Inti or Mama Quilla (Inca): sun and moon.
- Rainbow Serpent (Aboriginal Australian): only with great care and respect for a living tradition.
- Ganesha (Hindu): beginnings and obstacles. A second Hindu tradition after Lakshmi, so only if the user asks. He is implied in story 2 (Ganesh Rao, Year 4).
- Sedna (Inuit): only in a heavily softened form.
- Eshu and Ogun (Yoruba): a second Yoruba story after Oshun, with care for a living tradition.
- Buddhist stories: with care for a living tradition.
- Kali and Durga (Hindu): a third Hindu tradition, so only if the user asks, and with great care.
- Tengri (Mongolian): the sky.
- Mami Wata, Papa Legba, Yemaya: only with care for living traditions.

## Considered and declined

- **Moloch**: sources associate him with child sacrifice, so he was declined as unsuitable for children.
