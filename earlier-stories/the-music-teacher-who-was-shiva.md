The first time we saw Mr Shah was on a Wednesday, and he was sitting on the grass in the middle of the playground with his eyes shut.

He didn't move AT ALL. Not when the football rolled past him. Not when Freddie Ng shouted "OI, SIR!" Not even when a pigeon landed on his head.

Zainab Malik said that he was either ASLEEP or a STATUE, and Archie Bell said that statues don't wear lanyards.

Then Mr Shah opened one eye and said, "Good morning, everyone," and the pigeon flew off, and we all agreed it was the calmest thing we had ever seen.

## THE EVIDENCE

At lunch, Poppy Doyle said she had been paying attention to Mr Shah, and that she had a THEORY. We all leaned in.

She said, "He's SHIVA."

Freddie said that Shiva lives on a mountain, not in a school. But Poppy said that Mr Shah teaches music and the music room is on the TOP FLOOR, so that's basically a mountain with a lift.

That's when she showed us her list.

**1. HE SITS REALLY STILL.** Shiva meditates on a mountain for years. Mr Shah did it for twenty minutes and a pigeon.

**2. HE HAS A DRUM.** In the music cupboard there's a little drum shaped like an hourglass. Poppy had looked it up and Shiva has one just like it, and it's called a DAMARU.

**3. HE WEARS A BLUE SCARF.** Every day. Even when it's hot. And Shiva has a blue throat.

We all said "OHHH" together because it was a very good list.

## THE WET HAIR

On Thursday, Archie noticed something that made us all GASP.

Mr Shah's hair was DRIPPING.

It was not raining. There was not a cloud. But water was running down the back of his neck and Archie said that in the story, a whole RIVER comes out of Shiva's hair.

Zainab said that she'd seen a leaky tap in the staff room.

But Freddie said that was exactly what a god would WANT you to think.

## THE SNAKE

On Friday, a man came to our school with a box of reptiles for Science Week. He said that everyone should stay in their seats and not touch anything.

Then he took out a long, stripy snake, and it slid right out of his hands and across the floor, and every single person in Year 2 screamed.

The snake went straight past Miss Okoye, straight past the reptile man, and up the leg of Mr Shah, who was standing at the back. It wound itself round and round until it was draped across his shoulders, with its head resting on his neck.

Mr Shah did not scream. He did not jump. He did not even blink.

He said, "Hello, you," and gently patted the snake on the head.

The reptile man whispered, "She's never done that before."

Poppy turned to us with her eyes as wide as dinner plates and mouthed, **"A SNAKE. ON HIS NECK."**

## THE DANCING

On Monday, there was a special assembly, and Mr Shah was told to play some music.

He started by tapping his little hourglass drum, very softly. *Dum-dum, dum-dum.* Then a bit louder. Then faster. And all of a sudden, he began to DANCE.

He lifted one foot. He spun round. His arms went in all directions, and his scarf swirled, and his shoes went **THUMP THUMP THUMP** on the stage. We could actually feel the floor wobbling.

Nobody spoke. Even Mr Harris, the head teacher, stopped looking at his watch.

When the dancing finished, Mr Shah bowed with his hands together, and the whole school clapped for about three minutes.

Archie leaned over and whispered, "That was the Dance of the Universe."

Zainab said, "It was a very good dance."

Archie said, "THAT'S WHAT I'M SAYING."

## THE FAMILY

By Tuesday, there was so much evidence that we started looking for his FAMILY.

Poppy said that Shiva's wife is called PARVATI. So we checked the staff list on the wall.

And there, in the office, was the school secretary. Her name was **MRS PARVATI RAO**.

We all fell silent. Freddie said, "That's... a LOT."

Then Zainab remembered that Shiva has two sons, one with an elephant's head who loves sweets. And we all turned and looked at Ganesh Rao in Year 4, who was standing right there eating a laddoo, and who gave us a very confused look and said, "Why are you all staring at me?"

We said, "No reason."

Ganesh offered us a bit of his laddoo, and we all said no thank you because we didn't want to be rude to someone's son. Then Archie said yes because it looked REALLY good.

## THE SOUP

On Wednesday, the dinner ladies announced that they were trying something NEW.

It was called **MYSTERY GREEN SOUP**, and it was in a huge pot, and it was a colour we had never seen before. It was somewhere between a swamp and a crayon.

It smelled like a wet sock that had been left in a fridge. Nobody would go near it. Even Freddie, who eats ANYTHING, took a step back.

The dinner lady, Mrs Dunn, held up a ladle and said, "Come on, don't be shy! Who wants to try it?"

Nobody said a word. A few people started edging towards the door.

Then someone walked to the front. It was Mr Shah.

He took the ladle. He looked at the soup. He took a deep breath.

And then he DRANK IT.

## THE BLUE THROAT

The hall went completely silent.

Mr Shah stood there with his eyes closed and his hand pressed against his neck. He did not swallow. He did not breathe. His face went very still and very serious, and he stayed like that for a REALLY long time.

Poppy grabbed my sleeve and said in a whisper, **"HE'S HOLDING IT IN HIS THROAT. SO NOBODY ELSE HAS TO."**

Then Mrs Rao hurried across the hall and put her hand on his shoulder, very gently, like she knew exactly what to do.

At last, Mr Shah opened his eyes. He cleared his throat, a bit raspily, and he said, in a very calm voice:

**"Everyone, please. Do not eat the soup."**

The dinner ladies looked at each other. Then Mrs Dunn quietly took the pot away and poured it down the sink, and nobody ever mentioned it again.

## THE ENDING

At home time, we all went up to Mr Shah and shouted, "THANK YOU!"

He looked surprised. "What for?"

"For the soup," said Poppy.

He smiled. "Oh, that. It was only a bit of soup."

And then he picked up his drum and walked away down the corridor with Mrs Rao beside him, and Ganesh walked behind them, eating something sticky.

Archie said that none of the adults would EVER admit it, and that was how you knew.

Freddie said that Mr Shah had been wearing a **normal blue scarf**.

And Poppy said, "Yes. Exactly. *Normal.*"

**The End.**
