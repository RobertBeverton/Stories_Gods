The first time we saw Mrs Felicity was on a Monday, and she WON THE RAFFLE.

It was the school fair, and the prize was a giant teddy bear. Mr Harris pulled a ticket out of the bucket and read it out, and it was her. She said, "Oh, how lovely!" like it was a surprise.

Then he pulled out a ticket for the second prize, a hamper of biscuits. It was her AGAIN.

Then the third prize. A voucher for a pizza. HER.

Nobody said a word. Mr Harris looked in the bucket to check it wasn't all her tickets, and it wasn't. It was just regular tickets.

Mrs Felicity gave the teddy to a Reception boy who was crying, shared out the biscuits, and gave the voucher to the lollipop man. Then she went home. We all just stood there with our mouths open.

## THE NEW TEACHING ASSISTANT

On Tuesday, she walked into our classroom and Miss Riley said, "This is Mrs Felicity, and she's going to be helping us for the rest of term."

Felix Bailey, who sits next to me, whispered, "Is that the RAFFLE LADY?"

It was.

She had a big basket with her, shaped like a curly horn, and it was spilling over with apples, grapes, little plums and a banana. She said that it was for anyone who needed a snack.

Maya Singh put her hand up and said, "Is it magic?"

Mrs Felicity said, "It's just a very full basket, dear."

But Maya said it again to us at break. "It was ALREADY full. It didn't have anything in it when she walked in. I SAW her walk in."

I had seen her walk in. It had definitely been full from the start. We agreed that was weird.

## WHO IS FELICITAS?

Maya is the only person in our class who has read a whole book about Roman gods, and she told us she had a THEORY.

She said, "I think Mrs Felicity is FELICITAS."

Nobody knew who that was, so Felix put up his hand like we were in class, and said, "Who?"

Maya took a deep breath. She said that two thousand years ago there was a huge empire called ROME, in Italy, with roads and soldiers and big stone buildings, and the Romans believed in LOTS of gods. And one of them was Felicitas.

She was the goddess of good luck, success and happiness. When something went well, when the crops grew or a baby was born or someone got a really nice surprise, the Romans said it was thanks to Felicitas. She even had her picture on their coins.

We all said "OHHH".

Then Maya said, "And the Romans spoke Latin. And in Latin, the word for lucky and happy is **FELIX**."

Everybody turned and looked at Felix.

Felix said, "That's MY NAME."

Maya said, "I know."

Felix went very quiet, and then a very wide smile spread right across his face. He said, "Do you mean my name means LUCKY?"

Maya said, "Yes. And Felicitas is like, your goddess."

Felix sat up very straight for the rest of the day.

## THE EVIDENCE

Maya said that every good theory needs a list.

**1. THE BASKET.** Felicitas is always drawn holding a curly horn overflowing with fruit and flowers. It's called a CORNUCOPIA, and it means there's always enough for everyone.

**2. THE UMBRELLA.** Mrs Felicity carries a long umbrella with two ribbons twisted round the handle. Maya said that Felicitas holds a staff with two SNAKES twisted round it and a pair of tiny wings at the top. We all looked, and the umbrella had a small silver wing on it. Ollie Grant said it was a bird. Maya said it was the same thing.

**3. THE WINNING.** She won the raffle three times. She won the tombola, too, and then she won a game of Snap against the whole of the class above at once.

**4. THE CLOVERS.** Her coat is covered in four-leaf clovers, and there are four hundred on it at least. Priya Nair said it looked like someone had thrown a lawn at her.

We agreed it was a really good list.

## THE LUCKY DAY

On Wednesday, Felix decided to do an EXPERIMENT.

He said that if Mrs Felicity was his goddess, he should get extra lucky if he stood next to her. So all day, he stuck to her like glue.

When she walked to the hall, he walked to the hall. When she went to the staff room, he stood outside the door. When she went to the toilets, Miss Riley told him to get back to his seat.

But something remarkable happened. He found a pound coin. He got to the front of the lunch line. He got the last chocolate sponge, even though it had been on the very back shelf. He got picked first for football AND for the egg and spoon race, and he won the egg and spoon race without dropping a single egg.

By home time, Felix was walking about two inches off the ground.

He said, "I think I'm the luckiest person in the WORLD."

And that's when Mrs Felicity looked at him, and smiled very gently, and said something we didn't expect.

## THE LESSON

She said, "Luck is a lovely thing, Felix, but it isn't always the whole story."

Felix said, "What do you mean?"

She said, "Well, you found the coin. What did you do with it?"

Felix said, "I put it in my pocket."

She said, "And when you got to the front of the lunch line, did you think about the people at the back?"

Felix went a bit pink. "No."

She said, "And when you got the last chocolate sponge, was anyone else looking forward to it?"

Felix looked down. "Yes. Ollie. He's been asking for it for three days."

She nodded. "Felicitas was never just about *getting* lucky. She was about being **happy**, and a really happy person shares. The best luck is the kind that makes other people feel lucky, too."

Felix thought about that for a very long time.

Then he walked over to Ollie and gave him half of his chocolate sponge.

Ollie said, "Really?"

Felix said, "Yes. It was your turn."

We all agreed it was the nicest moment of the term. And Mrs Felicity gave a little nod, and her basket gave a tiny shake, and an extra apple popped out of the top and rolled down the corridor.

## THE NEW PE TEACHER

But on Thursday, things got a bit weird.

We went into the hall for PE, and there was a NEW teacher, standing next to a big wooden wheel on a stand. It was as tall as Freddie from the top class, painted in red and gold, and it had a pointer at the top. Written on the segments were things like: **"DODGEBALL", "SACK RACE", "TIDY THE CUPBOARD", "FREE PLAY",** and **"EVERYONE HOPS."**

She was tall and elegant with long dark hair. She wore a scarf over her eyes, which was a bit strange, but she walked perfectly straight and said, "Good morning, everybody!" in a loud, bright voice. Her name badge said **MS FORTUNE**.

Maya went completely pale. She grabbed my arm.

She said, "THAT'S **FORTUNA.**"

## WHO IS FORTUNA?

Maya whispered fast.

She said that Fortuna is Felicitas's sister. She's another Roman goddess of luck, but she's the **wild** sort. Felicitas gives you the nice, happy, shared luck. Fortuna is **chance**, the luck that can be good or bad, and nobody knows which. The Romans drew her with a WHEEL, because she turned it, and when it spins, you could go UP or you could go DOWN.

Sometimes she's shown wearing a blindfold, because luck doesn't look at who it gives itself to. It just spins.

Ollie said, "So she's chaos?"

Maya said, "She's **fun** chaos."

Ms Fortune clapped her hands and shouted, "Right then! Who wants to spin the wheel?"

## THE WHEEL

Felix put up his hand. He had been feeling lucky all week.

He walked up, took hold of the wheel, and gave it the BIGGEST spin of his life. It went **whirr, whirr, whirr, clack, clack, clack, clack...** and slowed... and slowed... and finally stopped on...

**TIDY THE CUPBOARD.**

We all looked at it. Felix sat down.

"Oh well," said Ms Fortune cheerfully. "That's the wheel for you!"

So we spent the whole of PE tidying the PE cupboard, which had four hundred bean bags in it and a smell like a hamster's cage.

But then Maya spun it, and it landed on **FREE PLAY**. And Ollie spun it, and got **SACK RACE**. And Priya spun it, and got **EVERYONE HOPS**, and we all hopped round the hall for ten minutes shouting.

By the end, we had been up AND down, and sometimes in the middle, and it was REALLY fun, and we couldn't decide whether we were lucky or unlucky. We just knew it was quite a good way to spend an hour.

## THE SISTERS

At home time, we saw Mrs Felicity and Ms Fortune standing together at the gates.

They looked very alike, apart from the scarf, and they were arguing in a friendly way.

Mrs Felicity said, "You've been spinning that wheel on children again."

Ms Fortune said, "It's called a lesson, Fel."

Mrs Felicity said, "It's called mischief."

Ms Fortune grinned. "You and your baskets. Somebody has to keep things interesting."

Then they both noticed us all standing there.

Felix stepped forward. His voice wobbled a bit. He said, "Mrs Felicity? Is it true that my name means lucky?"

Mrs Felicity bent down and smiled.

"It does," she said. "But I'll tell you a secret, Felix. Luck goes up, and luck goes down, and my sister is very good at making sure of it. But a **Felix**, a really happy person, is someone who's kind when the wheel is high, and brave when it's low. That's the sort of luck that stays with you."

Felix nodded. "I think I can do that."

She said, "I know you can."

And then Ms Fortune put out her hand and said, "Fancy a spin?"

Felix looked at her, and looked at the wheel, and said, "Yes please."

He spun it, and it landed on **ICE CREAM FOR EVERYONE.**

We all looked at Ms Fortune. She said, "Well, I didn't plan THAT."

And then, from nowhere, an ice cream van came round the corner playing a tune.

**The End.**
