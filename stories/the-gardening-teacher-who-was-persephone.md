The first time we saw Miss Persey was on a Monday, and the car park was GROWING.

Nobody believed us at first. But when she got out of her little yellow car and walked across the tarmac, tiny flowers popped up behind her, one after another, in all the cracks. There were daisies, buttercups, and a very confused dandelion.

Mr Harris looked at the flowers. Then he looked at his shoes. Then he said, "Must be the weather."

It was October. It was FREEZING.

Miss Persey was the new leader of Gardening Club. She had long brown hair with a flower crown that she said she'd "just thrown together", and she hummed when she walked. She also looked a little bit sad, like someone who's had a lovely holiday and is thinking about the end of it.

## THE MUM

On Tuesday, we noticed the lady at the fence.

She stood on the other side of the school field every morning, wearing a big green coat with huge pockets full of seeds, watching Miss Persey through the bars. Every time Miss Persey went inside, she called out, "PERRRSY! COAT!"

Miss Persey would call back, "MUM! I'M FINE!"

The lady's name was Mrs Demmer. She ran the allotments next door, where she grew the biggest marrows anyone had ever seen. She also never blinked, and Ollie said that was a bit scary.

Felix Bailey said, "She just really loves her daughter."

Ollie said, "She's watching her like a hawk."

Felix said, "That's what loving someone looks like sometimes."

Then his twin brother, Alex, who had been quietly eating an apple, said, "And she keeps seeds in her pockets so she can look after the whole world, even when she's worried."

We all went quiet, because both of those were true, and also quite deep for a Tuesday.

## WHO IS PERSEPHONE?

At break, Maya Singh pulled us behind the compost bin. Maya is the one who read the whole book about Roman gods, and then, when she finished it, she read the Greek one too. So we know to listen.

She said, "Miss Persey is PERSEPHONE."

Kenji Mori said, "Is that a type of biscuit?"

Maya said, "NO, Kenji."

She explained that a very long time ago, in a country called Greece, people believed in lots of gods who lived at the top of a mountain called Olympus. Persephone is the goddess of spring. Wherever she walks, flowers grow.

Her mum is called **Demeter**, and Demeter is the goddess of the harvest, which means she makes the crops grow, like wheat, apples and marrows. She loves Persephone more than anything in the whole world.

Ollie said, "So Mrs Demmer is Demeter."

Maya said, "OBVIOUSLY."

## THE LIST

Maya said that you can't go around saying people are gods without a list. Alex had the pencil, and Felix held the notebook, because they always share jobs.

**1. THE FLOWERS.** They grow wherever she walks. On Wednesday, she walked down the corridor and the lino sprouted a tulip.

**2. THE HUMMING.** Every time she hums, the weeds in the playground stand up straighter.

**3. THE MUM.** Mrs Demmer calls her Persy, and she's got seeds in every pocket.

**4. THE SADNESS.** Maya said that Persephone has a secret, and it's the sort of secret that makes you look sad at the end of a nice day. But she wouldn't tell us what it was yet.

We agreed it was the strongest list since Mr Thorley.

## GARDENING CLUB

On Wednesday, we all went to Gardening Club.

Miss Persey gave each of us a small pot and a seed. Mine was a bean. Felix's was a sunflower. Alex's was a pumpkin seed. Ollie's was something that looked a bit like gravel.

She said, "Now. Does anybody know what seeds do first?"

Kenji said, "Grow?"

Miss Persey smiled. "Not first. First, they sit in the dark. For a long time. Under the soil, where it's cold and quiet and nothing seems to be happening."

Ollie said, "That sounds boring."

Alex put up his hand. "I don't think it's boring. I think it's like when you're asleep. You look like nothing's happening, but your body's growing."

Felix said, "Or like when you're learning to ride a bike and you keep falling off, but you're secretly getting better."

Miss Persey looked at them both and her whole face lit up.

She said, "Exactly. The dark isn't the end. It's the **beginning**. Every flower you've ever seen started in the dark."

We all looked at our seeds. Mine suddenly seemed a lot more important.

She said, "I'd like you to say something with me. It's a good thing to say when things are hard. Ready?"

We said we were.

She said it slowly, and we all said it back:

**"I AM A SEED. THE DARK IS WHERE I GROW."**

It felt very odd, saying it in the middle of the school field. But it also felt quite good, like putting on a warm jumper.

## THE FLOWER

That's when it happened.

At the end of the club, Miss Persey wandered across the field to put the watering cans away. We saw her stop. She bent down to pick a flower.

It was the most beautiful flower any of us had ever seen. It was a deep, shiny purple with a hundred petals, and it glowed slightly. It was growing right in the middle of the grass, even though we were quite sure it hadn't been there a minute ago.

Maya said, quietly, "Don't pick it."

But Miss Persey was too far away to hear. She picked it.

And the ground **cracked**.

## THE CRACK

It began as a thin black line. Then it zigzagged across the grass, wider and wider, with a deep, rumbling **GRRRRRRRR**, and then, with a loud **KRAK**, the whole field split open like a biscuit.

Out of the crack came a cold wind that smelled of damp stones. And out of the dark, we saw something rising: a black chariot, pulled by four jet-black horses, with a man sitting in the front.

We couldn't see his face. He wore a woolly hat pulled right down over his eyes, and he had a long dark coat.

He didn't say anything. He reached down, picked up Miss Persey as gently as you'd pick up a kitten, and put her in the seat beside him.

She dropped the flower. She said, "Oh!"

Then the horses leaped back down into the crack, and the earth **SLAMMED** shut behind them.

There was nothing left but a smooth patch of grass.

## WINTER

We were still standing there with our mouths open when we heard the scream.

It was Mrs Demmer, coming through the gate at a run. She stared at the patch of grass. She stared at us. She didn't say anything. Her whole face crumpled, and she gave a great, deep, wobbling cry that sounded like a broken heart.

And the world went cold.

It began with the leaves. Every leaf on every tree turned brown at the same time, and fell. The flowers curled up. The marrows in the allotment shrank, wrinkled, and went black. The grass crunched, and then it was white with frost, and then it started to snow, in the middle of the afternoon, in October.

In the dinner hall, the potatoes turned to stone. Mrs Ibbs wrote **"HIGHLY UNUSUAL"** on a menu.

Mr Harris said, "It can't be winter. It's only half past two."

Mrs Demmer sat in the middle of the playground with her arms wrapped around her knees. She said, in a very small voice, "I can't make anything grow. Not until she's home."

## THE PLAN

Nobody knew what to do. Every grown-up was running about and shouting, "Check the boiler!"

We stood in a little group in the snow, shivering, trying to think.

Maya said, "I know where she's gone."

We looked at her.

She took a deep breath. "In the stories, Persephone is picked up by **HADES**."

Ollie said, "Is he a bad guy?"

Maya said, "He's the king of the **Underworld**. It's a deep, dark place under the earth where people go when they die. He looks after it. He's not evil. He's fair and he's very rich, because everything shiny in the ground belongs to him, like gold and diamonds. But he's really **lonely**. Nobody ever visits him."

Kenji said, "So he took her because he wanted company?"

Maya said, "Yes. But he didn't **ASK** her. And that's the problem."

Felix said, "That's a big problem. You have to ask."

Alex said, "And you have to listen to the answer."

Ollie said, "So how do we get to the Underworld?"

Maya said, "There's usually a door."

Slowly, we all had the same idea. And together we said, "THE BOILER ROOM."

## THE DOOR

The boiler room is in the basement. It's where a whole class got lost on the day we met Mr Jackson. The door is heavy and grey, and it's always locked, and it's always **really cold**.

That afternoon, it was open.

There was a gap, just wide enough for us. A cool, dark breeze drifted through. And at the bottom of the stairs, something was glowing, very faintly, like a lantern.

Maya said, "Right. Everyone listen. It's going to be scary. We might feel like turning around. That's okay. But Miss Persey's down there, and so is the whole of the spring. We have to go."

Felix said, "What if I'm too scared?"

Alex said, "Then I'll hold your hand. And if I get scared, you can hold mine."

Felix thought about that. Then he nodded, and the twins held hands, one each side of the doorway.

We said, very quietly, the thing Miss Persey taught us:

**"I CAN BE SCARED AND BRAVE AT THE SAME TIME."**

And we went down the stairs.

## THE STAIRS

It was a very long staircase. It felt like it went down much further than the school.

The walls were made of black stone, and every few steps, a little blue flame flickered in a hole in the wall. Our breath came out in clouds. Somewhere, far away, water was dripping.

Ollie clutched the back of my jumper. Kenji clutched Ollie. Alex and Felix walked at the front, side by side, with their chins up, even though both their knees were knocking.

At the bottom, there was a huge iron gate, with bars as thick as my arm. And in front of the gate, curled up, was **THE DOG**.

## THE DOG

It was the size of a van. It was black, with fur like wet coal. And it had **THREE HEADS**.

All of them were asleep, and they were snoring in different rhythms. *Huff-huff. Wheeeeze. Snrrk.*

Maya's voice was barely a whisper. "That's Cerberus. He guards the gate so that nobody can leave the Underworld."

Kenji said, "He's got three heads."

Maya said, "I know."

Kenji said, "That's a LOT of teeth."

Maya said, "I KNOW."

Just then, the middle head opened one eye. Then the left head woke up, and sniffed. Then the right. All three noses twitched, and all three pairs of eyes turned to look at us. One of them gave a very deep growl, which shook the floor.

Ollie whispered, "Run?"

Felix said, in a trembling voice, "Wait."

Alex said, "I think he's hungry."

## THE SANDWICHES

The twins looked at each other, and they didn't need to say anything else.

Felix said, "The Playground Dog. In Wigglesbottom, Susie Keys gave him her cheese sandwich, and he stopped running and became really friendly."

Maya whispered, "That was a story, Felix."

Alex said, "I know. But I think all dogs are the same. They just want a sandwich."

Then the twins did the bravest thing I've ever seen anyone do. Felix took out his lunchbox and held out his cheese sandwich to the middle head, with his hand shaking so much that the cheese rattled. At exactly the same time, Alex took out his ham sandwich and held it out to the left head, with his hand shaking just as much.

They whispered the words together: **"I CAN BE SCARED AND BRAVE AT THE SAME TIME."**

Two heads leaned forward. Four nostrils sniffed. Two enormous mouths opened.

**GULP. GULP.**

Both sandwiches were gone.

But the right head was still growling, because it hadn't had anything. So Ollie, who was shaking so much he could hardly stand, held out a packet of crisps, and the right head ate the crisps, packet and all.

For a moment, nothing happened. Then the three tails began to wag, all together, so hard that the whole dungeon shook. The left head licked Alex's face. The middle head licked Felix's face. The right head licked Ollie's face and sneezed.

Kenji said, in a very high voice, "They just wanted to be fed."

Maya said, "Everyone does."

## THE WHISPERS

We went through the gate, which creaked open on its own.

On the other side was a long, dark hallway, with no end. Shadows crawled along the walls and ceilings, and they didn't look like the shadows of anything.

Then the shadows began to **whisper**.

*"You're too small…"*

*"You'll never find her…"*

*"Go home…"*

*"You're not clever enough…"*

*"Nobody will notice if you give up…"*

It was horrible. The voices crept into our ears and tugged at our thoughts. Ollie stopped walking. Kenji started to cry, very quietly. I felt a heavy, cold feeling in my stomach.

The shadows got **bigger**, and **louder**, and they started to close in.

And then Alex, who had gone very pale, stood up straight and said in a clear voice:

**"I CAN DO HARD THINGS."**

The shadows hissed.

Felix stepped up beside him and said, **"I AM SMALL, BUT I AM STRONG."**

The whispers got quieter.

Maya said, **"I AM A SEED. THE DARK IS WHERE I GROW."**

Ollie wiped his eyes, took a deep breath, and said, **"I AM BRAVE EVEN WHEN I'M SCARED."**

And then Kenji, who was still crying, said, **"I AM NOT ALONE."**

We all joined hands and said them again, together, as loud as we could.

The shadows shrank. They flickered, and dwindled, and slid back into the walls like water down a drain. The whispers faded to nothing, and the hallway was just a hallway, with a little blue lantern at the end.

Alex said, "That worked."

Felix said, "Of course it did. They're the truest things we know."

## THE KING

At the end of the hall was a room with a high ceiling full of glittering stones. There were diamonds the size of footballs, and rubies the size of apples, and gold that shone like honey. In the middle was a long black table with two chairs.

In one chair sat Miss Persey. She was holding a small, red fruit, with its skin split open to show hundreds of shiny seeds like tiny jewels.

In the other chair sat a man in a long black coat, with a woolly hat pulled down over his eyes. He looked up as we came in. He took off the hat.

Underneath, he was quite ordinary. He had a pale, tired face, with kind grey eyes and a nose that was a bit red, like he had a cold. His badge said **MR HAYDES, BOILER MAINTENANCE.**

He said, in a quiet, sad voice, "Oh. Visitors."

He looked genuinely surprised, as though he hadn't had visitors for a very long time.

## THE TALK

For a moment, we were all too nervous to speak. Then Felix and Alex took a step forward together.

Felix said, "Excuse me. Mr Haydes. Did you ask Miss Persey if she wanted to come?"

Mr Haydes looked down at his hands. He said, "No."

Alex said, "That wasn't very kind."

Mr Haydes said, "I know. I've been thinking about it all afternoon." He sighed. "I didn't mean to scare anyone. I've lived down here a very long time. It's quiet. And I saw a girl with flowers, and I thought, 'She must be wonderful.' But I'm not very good at talking to people. I thought if I just brought her here, she might stay."

Felix said, "It doesn't work like that."

Alex said, "But being lonely is really hard. If you want a friend, you can just ask someone. Even if you're scared they'll say no."

Mr Haydes stared at him. He said, "That sounds terrifying."

Alex said, "It is. But you can be scared and brave at the same time."

Mr Haydes said, "I'm beginning to see that."

Miss Persey spoke up. She said, "Actually, he's been very polite since I arrived. He gave me soup. And he said sorry about eight times."

Mr Haydes went pink.

She said, "But I miss my mum. And the sun. And I can hear Mum crying all the way down here, and the flowers are dying, and it's all my fault."

Felix said, "It's **not** your fault."

Alex said, "It's definitely **his** fault."

Mr Haydes said, "It's definitely my fault."

## THE POMEGRANATE

Then Miss Persey looked down at the red fruit, and her face fell.

She said quietly, "There's another problem."

Maya gasped. "The pomegranate!"

Miss Persey said, "I was so hungry. I ate six of the seeds before I remembered the rule."

Ollie said, "What rule?"

Maya said, in a whisper, "In the Underworld, if you eat the food, you have to stay. One month for each seed. It's an old, old law that even Hades can't break."

We all looked at Mr Haydes. He gave a helpless shrug.

He said, "It really is the rule. I didn't make it."

Miss Persey's eyes filled with tears. "So I'll be stuck here for six months, and it'll be winter all that time, and Mum will be so sad, and the farmers, and the fields, and..."

She stopped.

## THE IDEA

Alex said, "Wait. Six seeds means six months, right?"

Felix said, "So that's only *half* the year."

Alex said, "So the other half..."

Felix said, "She can go home."

They grinned and said it together:

**"IT CAN BE BOTH!"**

Everybody turned to look at them. Kenji gave them two thumbs up.

Felix said, "She can have two homes. One down here, one up there."

Alex said, "And she can visit both, and be the queen of both."

There was a very long silence.

Mr Haydes slowly said, "I… hadn't thought of that."

Miss Persey said, "I could be with you in winter, when everything is sleeping and the world needs a quiet time. And I could be with Mum in spring and summer, when everything's growing."

She looked around at the glittering room. She looked at Mr Haydes, who was holding his woolly hat, looking a bit hopeful and a bit scared. She looked at us.

She said, "I think I can love two places at once."

She turned to Mr Haydes. "But I'd like you to ask me properly this time. If I'm going to come back every winter, I want it to be because I want to, not because I was taken."

Mr Haydes stood up very straight. He took a deep breath, the sort you take before jumping into a cold swimming pool.

He said, "Persephone. Would you do me the honour of coming to stay with me for the winter?"

She smiled the first real smile we'd seen all day.

"Yes," she said. "Thank you for asking."

## THE WAY HOME

We had one more problem. The way back up would take hours, and it was nearly home time.

Mr Haydes snapped his fingers. A gust of wind swirled through the room. And through the glittering door came a young man, running **very fast**, with little silver wings on his trainers and a satchel over his shoulder.

He said, "Parcel for the Underworld? I'm Hermes. Oh. No parcel. Lift for six children and a goddess? I can do that."

Then he looked at the twins and frowned. "Hang on. Which one of you is Felix?"

"Me," said Felix and Alex together.

Hermes said, "Right. I'm just going to carry you both and not worry about it."

Before anyone could say anything else, he grabbed all of us, and **WHOOOSH**, we were flying. Up the stairs, through the gate, past the three-headed dog (who barked three happy barks), and out of the boiler room into the snow.

When we landed, Hermes tipped an invisible cap, said "Delivery complete," and vanished in a blur of silver.

## SPRING

The field cracked open again, and out stepped Miss Persey, holding a pomegranate and wearing her flower crown.

Mrs Demmer, who was still sitting in the playground, looked up. Her mouth opened. She jumped to her feet and ran, and the two of them hugged for about six minutes.

And **everything bloomed**. The leaves burst back onto the trees. Daffodils shot up out of the snow. Marrows swelled in the allotment until they were the size of small cars. The potatoes in the dinner hall turned back into potatoes, and Mrs Ibbs crossed out **HIGHLY UNUSUAL** and wrote **BACK TO NORMAL.**

Mr Harris stood in the playground, wiping his glasses. He said, "Well, that was a funny sort of week."

Nobody told him.

## THE DEAL

Later, Miss Persey knelt in front of us and said, "Thank you. You were so brave. Do you know what you did?"

We shook our heads.

She said, "You listened to the scared part of yourselves, and you went on anyway. You fed a very hungry dog. You used your words to chase away the dark. And you found a kind answer when everybody else only saw a problem."

She looked at Alex and Felix. "Especially you two. You looked after each other the whole way, and that's the bravest thing there is."

The twins grinned at each other.

She said, "I'll be leaving again in the autumn. Mum knows. It's okay. She's learning to let me go, and I'm learning to come back. It's how the seasons work, you see. Winter isn't a punishment. It's a long sleep, where seeds get ready to grow. And then I come home, and the spring comes with me."

She handed each of us a tiny pot with a seed. Mine was a poppy.

She said, "Remember what to say when it's dark."

We said it, together:

**"I AM A SEED. THE DARK IS WHERE I GROW."**

## THE POSTMAN

On the way home, Maya noticed something odd.

The postman was standing at the school gate. He had a satchel, a red jacket, and a very big smile. But his shoes were very strange. They were bright white trainers with little silver wings on the heels, which flapped slightly, even though there was no wind.

He said, "Afternoon, kids. Got a parcel for a Mr Thorley, one for a Mrs Ibbs, and a very urgent one for the new PE teacher."

Maya whispered, "That's HERMES."

Felix whispered, "He's the delivery man for the gods."

Alex whispered, "And he said a **PE teacher**."

And that's when we all looked towards the gym. The doors were open, and inside, something small and furry was bouncing up and down on the climbing frame, shrieking, "Is that a MANGO?"

**The End.**
