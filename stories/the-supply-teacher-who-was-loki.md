The first time we saw Mr Locke was on a Tuesday, and Miss Riley was ILL.

We were all quite pleased about that, until Mr Harris came in and said, "Class, this is Mr Locke. He's your supply teacher." Then Mr Harris looked at the floor and said, "Good luck."

Nobody knew why he said that.

Mr Locke was thin, with a long smiley face and very bright green eyes. He wore a green jumper with a gold stripe, and he said, "Hello, my favourites!" which seemed a bit much, because he'd never met us.

Then he sat on Miss Riley's chair. It made a loud **PARRRRP** noise.

He looked VERY surprised. He said, "Goodness, who did that?"

But we ALL saw his hand go under the cushion first.

## THE FIRST CLUE

At break, Dev Okafor called a meeting by the bins. Dev is the one who worked out that Mr Thorley was THOR, so we all knew to listen.

He said, "Something is very weird about Mr Locke. Did you see his eyes when he sat on the whoopee cushion? They twinkled."

Ruby Finch said, "Everybody's eyes twinkle."

Dev said, "Not like THAT. It was a **TROUBLE twinkle**."

Then he said, "I think he's LOKI."

None of us knew who that was, except Priya Nair, who said, "Is he Thor's brother?" Dev said, "Sort of. Not exactly. It's complicated." And then he explained.

## WHO IS LOKI?

Dev said that Loki is a god from the old Norse stories, who lived in a place called Asgard, up in the sky. He is the god of TRICKS.

He said Loki can change into anything he wants. A bird, a fish, a horse, a mouse, an old lady. He's cleverer than almost everyone. And he can't stop himself from playing pranks.

Ollie Grant said, "Is he BAD?"

Dev said, "No. He's more like... a friend who sometimes goes TOO FAR."

He said that Loki and Thor go on adventures together. Loki once cut off the golden hair of Thor's wife, Sif, as a joke, and Thor got SO cross that Loki had to get dwarves to make her new hair out of REAL GOLD. Another time, Loki borrowed a magic feather cloak and turned into a falcon. And once, he dressed up in a bridesmaid's dress to help Thor get his hammer back from a giant.

Felix Bailey said, "I remember that one! Thor ate a whole OX."

Dev said, "Yes. He's a good friend, but he can be a bit of a MENACE."

We all looked back at the classroom window. Mr Locke was inside, balancing a ruler on his nose.

## THE LIST

Dev said that we needed evidence. Priya had her notebook.

**1. HE CAN'T STOP PRANKS.** Within one hour, Mr Locke had swapped the labels on the pencil pots, made the clock go backwards (it was a mirror) and told Archie Bell that his shoes were on the wrong feet. Archie had checked three times.

**2. HE IS IN TWO PLACES AT ONCE.** At 11 o'clock, he was in our classroom. At 11:01, Mr Harris saw him in the staff room eating a biscuit, and Mr Harris said that is NOT POSSIBLE.

**3. HE LOOKS DIFFERENT EVERY TIME.** At 9 o'clock his hair was dark. At lunchtime it was a bit red. After lunch it was shorter and he had glasses. Ruby said that people just do that. Dev said, "Not THAT quickly."

**4. HE WINKS.** He winks at EVERYTHING. At the door. At the hamster. At a very confused fire extinguisher.

We all agreed that the evidence was **STRONG**.

## THE HAMSTER

On Wednesday, we noticed something about our class hamster, Biscuit.

Biscuit is a normal, brown, fluffy hamster who runs on his wheel and sometimes falls asleep in his own food bowl. But on Wednesday morning, Biscuit had very bushy eyebrows. They were a bit too bushy for a hamster. And he was grinning.

And he was a little bit **bigger** than Biscuit. And he winked.

Priya whispered, "That's not Biscuit."

Dev whispered, "It's LOKI. He's turned into a HAMSTER."

Just then, Mr Locke walked in, with a cup of tea, and sat down, and said, "Good morning, everybody! Did I miss anything?"

Everyone looked from Mr Locke to the hamster. Then Biscuit, the real Biscuit, came out from behind the cage, looking VERY confused and very small, and ran onto Ollie's shoulder.

The hamster with the eyebrows had vanished.

## THE MANE

On Thursday, something went too far.

Our school has a toy lion called **SIR LIONEL**. He sits in the front hall and he's the school mascot. He has a magnificent golden mane, made of real wool, and the whole school loves him. Mr Harris gives him a little wave every morning.

On Thursday, Sir Lionel had NO MANE.

It was gone. He was just a bald, yellow lion with surprised eyes.

One of the little ones started crying. Mrs Tibbs gasped. Mr Harris held his own hair, like it might fall off too.

And then we heard a voice at the front door. It was a **VERY DEEP** voice, and it said, "WHO. HAS. DONE. THIS?"

It was Mr Thorley, the caretaker. He had come to fix a light. He was holding his hammer, and his red beard was **bristling**, and a small spark of lightning was fizzing at the end of his finger.

All of us went silent. Ollie whispered, "He's going to make it rain."

Dev whispered, "He's going to do more than that."

## MR LOCKE'S PLAN

At that moment, Mr Locke strolled out of the classroom and saw the bald lion.

He said, "Oh dear."

Mr Thorley turned around very slowly and stared at him. The hallway felt like it was about to explode. Mr Thorley said, in a very quiet voice, "Locke."

Mr Locke said, "Thorley! Long time. How are you? You look well. Have you been working out?"

Mr Thorley said, "Did you cut Sir Lionel's mane?"

Mr Locke said, "Me? No. I just... borrowed it. For a bit. As a joke. I was going to put it back."

Mr Thorley held up his hammer. There was a very big silence. A light bulb popped.

And then Mr Locke said something that surprised everyone.

He said, "I'm sorry."

We all gasped.

He said, "I went too far. I thought it'd be funny, and then I saw the little girl crying, and I felt AWFUL. I'll fix it. I promise. Give me till the end of the day."

Mr Thorley looked at him for a very long time. Then he slowly put down his hammer, and said, "You've got until three o'clock."

## THE FIXING

We did NOT expect what happened next.

Mr Locke took a very deep breath, and for the first time that week, he looked completely serious. He went to the art cupboard and came out with **three rolls of gold tinsel**, a pot of glue, and a sparkly wig from the school play.

He asked us to help him.

Ollie held the lion. Priya cut the tinsel. Ruby stuck it on. Dev passed the glue, and Felix did the finishing touches. Mr Locke wove it, fluffed it and sprayed it with glitter. It took the WHOLE afternoon, and when we finished, Sir Lionel had the most glorious, shimmering golden mane that any lion had ever had.

It was **BETTER** than before.

We carried him back to the hall just as the clock hit three. The little girl looked up and squealed with delight.

Mr Harris said it was the best mane he had ever seen. Mrs Tibbs took a photo. And Mr Thorley stepped forward, looked carefully at the glittering lion, and said, quietly:

"Not bad, brother."

## THE ENDING

At home time, Mr Locke gave us all a very low bow.

He said, "Thank you for your help. I'm sorry for the whoopee cushion. And the mirror clock. And Archie's shoes."

Archie said, "My shoes WERE on the right feet?"

Mr Locke winked.

Then he said, "Remember, if you ever play a joke, make sure everyone's laughing. If they're not, it's not a joke any more. It's just being mean. And if you do go too far, say sorry, and make it right."

Priya wrote that down.

Then Mr Locke walked off down the corridor, whistling. But when we looked back, there was a little green **fish** swimming in the water fountain, with very bushy eyebrows.

Dev whispered, "Don't tell Mr Thorley."

We never did.

**The End.**
