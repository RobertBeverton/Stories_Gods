The first time we saw THEM was on a Monday, and they arrived in a MINIBUS shaped like a BOAT.

It was the front of the bus, honestly. It was pointy, painted red and gold, and it had a little flag on the top. Ollie Grant said it was the most embarrassing bus he had ever seen. Then seven people climbed out of it, one after another, and Mr Harris said, "Class, these are our new lunchtime staff," and Miss Riley dropped her coffee.

Nobody could tell why seven new lunchtime staff were needed, because the old dinner ladies had only left that morning, and they'd looked VERY confused about it.

## THE FISH

It was Monday, which meant the dinner was THE FISH.

We all sat down with sad faces. Even Felix, who is lucky, looked like he'd lost a pound coin.

But then the first new person came out from the kitchen. He was round and cheerful with a little pointy hat, and he was carrying a fishing rod AND a huge red fish under his arm, like a baby.

He was LAUGHING. He didn't even look at anyone. He just stood at the hatch, laughing at nothing, with a big rosy face.

His badge said **MR EBBIE**.

Priya said, "Excuse me, what's for dinner?"

Mr Ebbie leaned in and cupped his hand behind his ear and said, "EH?"

"WHAT'S FOR DINNER?"

"Ah! You'd like a SPOON?"

"NO. DINNER!"

"A SPOON of dinner! Of course!"

He gave her a spoonful of fish and a very large wink, and Priya looked at it, and tasted it.

Her eyes got very wide.

She said, "It's... NICE."

The whole hall went silent. Then forty people turned slowly and stared at THE FISH.

## THE KNOCKING

It turned out that Mr Ebbie couldn't hear very well, and the ONLY way to get his attention was to **KNOCK** on the counter.

Ollie found this out when he shouted, "CAN I HAVE CHIPS?" four times and Mr Ebbie just smiled and said, "Lovely weather!" Then Maya reached out and went **knock knock knock** on the hatch, and Mr Ebbie jumped, and said, "Ah! Yes! CHIPS! Coming up!"

After that, the whole school knocked on the hatch. It sounded like a hundred woodpeckers. Mrs Tibbs said it was the noisiest lunch in the history of the school, but she asked Mr Ebbie for seconds.

## WHO IS EBISU?

That's when Kenji Mori put up his hand. Kenji is the only person in our class whose gran lives in JAPAN, and he said she has a PICTURE on her wall that looks EXACTLY like Mr Ebbie.

He said, "That is EBISU."

We all said, "Who?"

Kenji said that Ebisu is a Japanese god of fishermen and good luck and honest, hard work. He is always smiling, because he's very happy, and he's always carrying a fishing rod and a big red fish called a sea bream.

And, Kenji said, in the old stories, Ebisu can't hear very well. So when people visit his temple, they knock on the wall to wake him up and get his attention.

Kenji said, "That is WHY everyone is knocking."

We all stopped and looked at our knuckles.

## THERE ARE SEVEN OF THEM

Kenji said that the REALLY big news was that Ebisu doesn't work alone. He's one of the **SEVEN LUCKY GODS**, who travel the world together on a huge ship full of treasure.

We all turned around and looked at the car park, where the boat-bus was parked.

He said that every New Year, they sail into harbours with gifts, and people hang up pictures of them for good luck. Kenji's gran puts the picture under her pillow so she'll have a good first dream.

Felix said, "I'm going to do that."

Ollie said, "How many have we found?"

We counted. We'd only met ONE. So that afternoon, we went hunting for the other six.

## NUMBER TWO

The second one was easy, because we heard him first. There was a CLUNK, a THUMP and a RATTLE, and a short man in a black hat came round the corner, dragging an ENORMOUS sack behind him.

It was the biggest sack we'd ever seen. He had a little wooden hammer in his other hand, and he sat down on a pile of rice bags.

His badge said **MR DAIKON**.

Kenji whispered, "DAIKOKU." He said that Daikoku is the god of wealth and farms and kitchens. He carries a sack that never runs out and a magic mallet, and he's always followed by mice.

We said, "Mice?"

Kenji said, "They're lucky."

We looked down and saw three small mice following Mr Daikon into the office, each with a tiny crumb.

Mr Daikon shook his sack, and out fell a lost glove, two lunchboxes, a recorder, and a cardboard crown that said **KING OF THE PLAYGROUND**.

He said, "Lost property," and handed it all back to its owners.

Ollie said, "I've been missing that glove since March."

## NUMBER THREE

The third was at the school gate, standing perfectly straight with a long pole in his hand, with a very fierce frown and a stern moustache. On top of his head was a small tower like a toy pagoda, and a belt of tiny orange cones.

He was the new lollipop man. He didn't say a word. He just stood there, staring straight ahead, guarding the road like it was a CASTLE.

His badge said **MR BISH**.

Kenji said, "BISHAMON. Warrior god. He guards against bad things, and he is VERY serious."

Ollie said, "He looks like he'd fight a bus."

And at that moment, a bus came round the corner, and Mr Bish raised his pole, and the bus stopped so quickly that all its passengers fell forward.

The driver said, "Sorry!"

Mr Bish said, "THANK YOU."

It was the only thing he said all week.

## NUMBER FOUR

The fourth was easy, because we could hear her playing music from the top of the stairs.

It was a lady with long, shiny hair and a lute, which is like a guitar, but rounder. She was sitting on a stool in the music room, plucking the strings in a way that made the whole room go quiet and floaty. Even the hamster stopped running on its wheel.

Her badge said **MS BENNY.**

Kenji whispered, "BENZAITEN. She's the only goddess of the seven. She's the goddess of music and the arts and flowing water."

Poppy said, "Is that why the tap in the music room doesn't drip any more?"

Ms Benny looked up and said, "It was dripping out of time."

We all nodded.

## NUMBER FIVE

The fifth one was HARD to miss.

His head was absolutely ENORMOUS. Not his head, exactly. His FOREHEAD. It was tall and round and shiny, like an egg, and it went up and up. He had a long white beard and a very small body, and he was carrying a scroll. He sat quietly in the corner of the library reading, with a crane, which is a tall bird, standing very still beside him.

Nobody had to ask. His badge said **MR FUKU.**

Kenji said, "FUKUROKUJU. God of wisdom and long life. In the pictures, his forehead is HUGE, because it's so full of BRAINS."

Maya said, "Can I ask him a question?"

She walked up and asked, "How do you spell 'necessary'?"

He said, without looking up, "One collar and two sleeves."

Maya wrote it down.

## NUMBER SIX

The sixth was very, very old and very, very slow, and he used a long wooden staff to walk. He had a white beard, a tiny smile, and a scroll tied to his staff.

He was sitting on a bench in the playground, feeding a very friendly white dog.

His badge said **MR JURY.**

Kenji said, "JUROJIN. Also a god of long life. He's about a thousand years old, but he's very relaxed."

Ollie said, "Is he the oldest?"

Kenji said, "He and Mr Fuku argue about it."

We peeked at the two of them in the playground, and Mr Fuku and Mr Jury were having a very polite fight.

Mr Fuku said, "I'm older."

Mr Jury said, "I'm older than that."

Mr Fuku said, "Not by much."

Mr Jury said, "By ENOUGH."

## NUMBER SEVEN

We couldn't find the seventh.

We looked in the hall, the gym, the dinner hall, the car park, and under the climbing frame. We looked for two whole days.

Then on Thursday, we found him sitting under a tree at the edge of the playground, laughing so hard that his enormous round belly was bouncing up and down like a beach ball.

He was surrounded by a crowd of Reception children, each holding a sticker.

He had a big cloth sack over his shoulder, and every time a child asked for something, he pulled out a sticker, a sweet, a toy or a balloon, and handed it over, still laughing.

His badge said **MR HOT.**

Kenji said, "HOTEI. He's the happiest of the lot. He has a big belly because he's always full of laughter, and he LOVES children."

Felix said, "Does he ever stop?"

Kenji said, "Not really."

Mr Hot saw us. He gave us each a sticker without asking, and we stuck them to our jumpers. Mine said **YOU ARE LUCKY**. Felix's said **YOU ARE FELIX**. Nobody knows how he knew.

## THE BIG PROBLEM

On Friday, there was a PROBLEM.

Mr Harris called a special assembly. He said, "Boys and girls, I'm sorry to say that the school is running out of money to heat the swimming pool."

(We have a tiny swimming pool. It's mostly used for storing the paddling pool.)

He said, "Without it, we can't hold the Summer Gala."

Everyone groaned. We love the Summer Gala. There's a sponge-throwing stall.

But then, at the back of the hall, something happened.

Mr Daikon picked up his sack and gave it one big shake. Out poured a small pile of gold coins. Mr Fuku calculated how much was needed and said it was exactly the right amount. Ms Benny played a tune that made every adult in the room feel very generous, and Mr Hot laughed so loudly that Mr Harris laughed too. Mr Bish stood at the door to make sure nobody left until the whole sum was collected, and Mr Jury had a cup of tea.

And Mr Ebbie knocked on the table and said, "Eh? Did someone say FISH PIE?"

He fried up forty fish pies and sold every single one at the gala, and they were so good that people queued round the car park. The pool was heated, the Gala happened, and Mr Harris said it was the luckiest week in the history of the school.

## THE DEPARTURE

On Saturday morning, we all went to school to say thank you.

But the car park was empty. The boat-bus was gone.

On the dinner hall door was a note. It was written in seven different handwritings, and it said:

*THANK YOU FOR YOUR KINDNESS. PLEASE PUT A PICTURE OF US UNDER YOUR PILLOW. IT HELPS.*

*P.S. (from Mr Ebbie) CAN YOU PLEASE KNOCK LOUDER NEXT TIME?*

We all stood there, looking at the note. Then Ollie looked up the road.

And we all saw it.

The boat-bus was at the end of the street, heading for the hill. But it wasn't driving, exactly. It was going **up and down**, up and down, like it was on waves. And the little flag on its roof had grown into a big, bright SAIL, filled with wind, even though there was no wind at all.

Seven small hands waved from the deck, and then it disappeared over the top.

Felix said, "I'm putting my picture under my pillow."

We all said, "Me too."

**The End.**
