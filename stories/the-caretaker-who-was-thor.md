The first time we saw the new caretaker was on a Thursday.

We were all in the playground when we heard a HUGE BOOM, and the ground WOBBLED, and a pigeon fell off the roof.

We all looked at the sky. But it was completely blue with NO CLOUDS. That's when Dev Okafor said, "That's not thunder. That's a MAN."

And it was. A very tall man with a bright red beard was standing next to a ladder he had just dropped. He picked it up and said, "Sorry, kids," in a voice so deep that Ruby Finch's water bottle rattled.

Then he walked away with a hammer swinging from his belt.

## THE NAME TAG

Priya Nair said we needed to find out his name, so at break we crept up and read his badge.

It said **MR THORLEY**.

We all GASPED, because on Monday, Mrs Tibbs had read us a story about a thunder god with a red beard and a hammer, and his name was THOR.

Dev said that Mr Thorley was one letter and a bit away from Thor and that it was NOT A COINCIDENCE. He said gods probably have to use fake names when they get jobs, in case people ask for autographs.

Ollie Grant said that Thor lives in the sky, not a school.

But Dev said that the sky doesn't have a very good pension.

## THE HAMMER TEST

Ruby Finch said that the true test of Thor was his hammer, because in the story it ALWAYS comes back to his hand.

So at lunchtime, we watched from the window. Mr Thorley was on a ladder, fixing a light. He put down his hammer, then turned around and said, "Now where did I put that?"

And Ollie whispered, "Don't look at it! It's about to fly back!"

We waited.

Mr Thorley bent down, picked it up off the floor, and said, "Ah! There you are."

Priya said that it had OBVIOUSLY flown back, and he'd just done it so fast that we missed it.

We couldn't argue with that.

## THE LUNCH

On Friday, we sat at the table next to him in the dining hall, and we all saw what he had for lunch.

It was a pie the size of a PILLOW.

He ate it in four bites. Then he ate a second pie. Then he drank an ENTIRE jug of water with his eyes closed, going **glug glug glug**.

Dev leaned over and whispered, "In the story he ate a WHOLE OX and EIGHT SALMON at a wedding."

We all stared at the empty pie tins, and everybody agreed that the evidence was **PILING UP**, and also that we were quite hungry.

## THE GOATS

Ollie Grant said that Thor travels in a chariot pulled by GOATS, so we should check the car park.

We rushed over at home time and looked. There was a small, rusty white van with a dent in it, and written on the side were the words:

**THORLEY & SONS: LOCKS, LADDERS AND LEAKS.**

Ruby said that it was a very clever disguise because nobody would EVER suspect a van.

But then Priya noticed a bag on the front seat with a picture on it of a goat, and it said GOAT CHEESE. And we all knew that we had solved it.

## A WARNING

At the weekend, I couldn't stop thinking about it. If Mr Thorley really WAS Thor, we had to be polite, because you don't want to get a thunder god cross. Dev said that if you make him angry, he'll make it rain on your birthday.

So on Monday, we all decided to be EXTRA GOOD. Ollie picked up every crisp packet he saw. Ruby held the door open for a LOT of people, including a bin. Priya gave Mr Thorley a drawing of him with a hammer and wrote underneath **"THANK YOU FOR PROTECTING US."**

Mr Thorley looked at the picture for a long time. Then he blinked a few times, and he said, "Well, I'll be. Nobody's ever drawn me before."

And we all noticed that his eyes looked a bit shiny, like when you're trying not to cry because something is too nice.

## THE GREAT STORM

On Thursday, the sky went a dark, grumbly grey, and Mrs Tibbs told us the school trip to the park was cancelled because of the weather.

We all groaned, but Dev put up his hand and said, "Mrs Tibbs, I think we should ask Mr Thorley to do something about the rain."

Mrs Tibbs said, "Why would we ask Mr Thorley to do something about the rain?"

And Dev said, "Because he's THOR."

There was a very long silence.

Then Mrs Tibbs began to laugh and she couldn't stop for about two minutes.

## THE TWIST

At home time, when it was almost dark, we were all standing at the gate, waiting for our mums and dads. Mr Thorley came out of the building and locked the door, and he looked up at the black clouds.

Then he said something to himself, very quietly. We were all close enough to hear.

He said, **"Not tonight, brother."**

We all froze.

Then he walked to his van, climbed in, and drove away.

And exactly TWO SECONDS later, the clouds went **BOOM**, and all the rain started to come down at once.

Dev Okafor turned to us, with the rain dripping off his nose, and said, **"I TOLD you."**

We stood there staring at the empty road, and Ruby whispered, "Who is his brother?"

Priya said, "Probably Loki."

And that's when we ALL looked around very quickly to see if anyone was standing near the school with a smirk and a green cloak. But there was only Mr Pickering, the lollipop man, who waved.

**The End.**
