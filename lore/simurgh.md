The Simurgh is an enormous, ancient and wise bird in Persian myth and in the epic Shahnameh, where it raises the hero Zal and gives him a feather to call it by. It is linked with healing and with knowing the world.

In a later Persian poem, The Conference of the Birds, thirty birds seek the Simurgh, and the name itself means "thirty birds".
