Pachamama, often translated as Mother Earth or Mother World, is honoured in the Andes of Peru, Bolivia, Ecuador and nearby regions. People make offerings to thank her, often before planting and sharing a meal, for example by pouring a little drink onto the ground first.

It is a living practice today, not only an old story.
