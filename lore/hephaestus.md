Hephaestus is the Greek god of metalworking, fire and craftsmanship. In myth he builds the gods' palaces, armour and many marvellous objects in his forge, including, in some tellings, golden helpers who move on their own.

He is usually described as lame in one or both legs, and he is the one Olympian who works with his hands. In one famous tale he is thrown from Olympus because of his foot (Hera does it in some tellings, Zeus in others), and later traps Hera in a golden throne. Some sources also give him Talos, the bronze guardian of Crete. Tellings vary on his parentage.
