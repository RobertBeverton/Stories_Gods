Oshun is an orisha, a spirit in the Yoruba religion of West Africa, linked with rivers, fresh water, love and sweetness. In the Nigerian city of Osogbo a sacred grove and river are dedicated to her.

Her worship crossed the Atlantic and lives on in the Caribbean and the Americas, so there are several rich traditions about her.
