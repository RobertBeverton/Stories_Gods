Raven is a central figure in the traditions of the Haida, Tlingit and other peoples of the Pacific Northwest coast. In many tellings he is a trickster and transformer who steals the light and brings it into a dark world.

These are living stories belonging to the peoples who tell them, and tellings differ between nations and families. This retelling is told with respect and is not a substitute for hearing them from those communities.

Find out more: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haida_mythology)
