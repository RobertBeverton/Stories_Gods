---
title: "Maui"
tradition: "Polynesian"
summary: "A clever trickster hero who slows the sun and fishes up islands."
stories: ["the-fisherman-who-slowed-the-sun"]
---

Maui is a hero and trickster across the Pacific, from Hawaii to Aotearoa New Zealand to Tonga. He is best known for slowing the sun so that the days were long enough to work in, and for pulling islands up from the sea with a magic fishhook.

Each island group tells his deeds a little differently. The details change from place to place, and that is part of how the stories live.
