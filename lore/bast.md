Bast (or Bastet) was an ancient Egyptian goddess of the home, protection and cats. She was shown as a cat or a woman with a cat's head, and her main centre of worship was the city of Bubastis.

Cats were valued for guarding stores of grain from mice, so a cat goddess was a goddess of a safe home.
