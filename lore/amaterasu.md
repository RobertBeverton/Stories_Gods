Amaterasu is the sun goddess in the Shinto tradition of Japan, honoured at the Grand Shrine of Ise. In the best known story she hides in a cave and the world goes dark, until the other gods make her curious with a joyful noise and a mirror.

The tale is told in two very old Japanese books, the Kojiki and the Nihon Shoki.
