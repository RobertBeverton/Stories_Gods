Baba Yaga is a figure from Slavic folktales, usually a witch who lives in a hut that stands on chicken legs deep in the forest. She can help or harm, and which one often depends on how politely a visitor behaves.

She is a character from folklore, not a god who was worshipped, and the stories about her are many and varied.
