---
title: "Lakshmi"
tradition: "Hindu"
summary: "The goddess of good fortune, welcomed with lamps at Diwali."
stories: ["the-lady-of-the-little-lamps"]
---

Lakshmi is the Hindu goddess of good fortune, prosperity and generosity. At Diwali, the festival of lights, families light small clay lamps and open their doors to welcome her in.

She is often shown with a lotus, a flower that grows from mud and stays clean, which is part of why it is linked with her.
