In Chinese myth Nüwa is a creator figure, shown with a woman's head and a serpent's body. One tale has her shaping the first people from yellow clay. Another has her melting coloured stones to patch a hole in the sky after a great disaster, propping it up with the legs of a giant turtle in some tellings.

The myths were written down over many centuries and the details differ between sources.
