---
title: "Nuwa"
tradition: "Chinese"
summary: "The goddess who mended the broken sky and shaped people from clay."
stories: ["the-lady-who-mended-the-sky"]
---

In Chinese myth Nuwa is a creator figure, shown with a woman's head and a serpent's body. One tale has her shaping the first people from yellow clay. Another has her melting coloured stones to patch a hole in the sky after a great disaster.

The myths were written down over many centuries and the details differ between sources.
