Coyote appears as a trickster and teacher in the stories of many Native American nations, and what he is like differs from nation to nation. He can be funny, greedy, clever and foolish, sometimes all in one tale.

These stories belong to the nations that tell them, so this is a retelling rather than a record of any one tradition.
