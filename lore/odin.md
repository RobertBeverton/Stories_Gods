Odin is the chief of the Norse gods, a god of wisdom, poetry and war, who gave up one eye for knowledge. His ravens Huginn and Muninn ("Thought" and "Memory") fly the world each day and report back, and his wolves are called Geri and Freki.

In our stories he is a gruff, kind, grandfatherly king who is willing to be told he is wrong.
